Angu & her paapa

It started with a monsoon and a new boy in class. It became forever on a September morning.

11 June 2012

The tall, lean boy at the back of the class

Already monsoon, and the sun had just started to rise past the rain. That was the day I saw a tall, lean boy who had come as a new admission into 11th standard. He joined my class. I was in Science, he was in Commerce. He sat in the last bench, and I sat right beside him.

I was an all in all kind of person. A backbencher when it came to enjoying school life, and a sort of middle bencher when it came to studies. He was a shy backbencher. I never knew then that the shy boy sitting next to me would one day become my better half, or that he would ever dance with me.

He was into football. I was into arts. I still remember cheering for him at the interschool competition. Back then he was just a friend, just a classmate.

the years in between

Laughter, trips and everything else

We were busy with our own lives, in school days filled with laughter, trips, school fests, annual day organising, dance practice, sports day, football matches. And somewhere in between all of it, quietly, our friendship grew.

It stayed with us when we moved on to the next level of our lives, into college, and without either of us noticing, we became best friends. A friend I could rely on. Someone I could tell anything to. Someone who never judged me. The kind of friend a girl only hopes for.

22 October 2019

A short trip to Areekal

Years went by, and then came a small trip to Areekal waterfall, where we came to know each other a little better.

On a calm evening, I took the leap. We were really good friends, best friends, the kind of friendship that could last a lifetime. So I asked him: why don't we get committed and live as one? Why don't you and me become us?

2 November 2019

And that was the day we became us.

Our first trip was to Ezhattumugham, and I still cherish it. And our life went on with little happinesses, fights, joy and fun, in a world of our own.

11 November 2023

The day you came home, formally

He visited my home with his parents for Pennukanal. The boy from the back bench, sitting in my living room, asking for me.

12 May 2024

19 March 2026

And then came Ishi

It was just you and me paapa..
our little world, our silly fights, our plans, our life.. ✨

And then came your annual leave… and somehow, that little break gave us the biggest surprise of our lives. ❤️

Two little lines, one tiny heartbeat, and suddenly there was a little Ishi waiting to become ours.

I still remember the look on your face when I showed you the positive. We had fought the night before, but in that moment, I saw something I'd never seen in your eyes before—happiness, disbelief, and a whole new sense of responsibility.

Then came the name hunting, the endless little conversations about what we'd call her, and all those times we talked to her while she was still inside me, she was already a part of our little world.

And then we heard her heartbeat for the first time… and I saw that look on your face all over again.

Every scan and check-up somehow became our little outings. Even when pregnancy got harder, you were right there with me. You went through two surgeries during my third trimester, and somehow, instead of feeling like we were going through two separate difficult things, it just brought us even closer. We had each other through it all.

And then came the day we finally met her.

You sat beside me through it all, even making me rest on your palm when I was exhausted. I don't remember the pain anymore. I remember you.

And when you held Ishi for the first time… the way you looked at her said everything. It was like you had fallen in love with her long before you even knew how much you could love her.

Watching you become her acha has been one of the most beautiful things I've ever seen.

We were just you and me.

And still, I would choose you

Through everything we’ve been through—the happiest days, the hardest ones, the silly fights, the big fights, the tears, the laughter, pregnancy, Ishi, and all the little moments in between…

I would still choose you.

There was even a time when our fights became so much that our families wondered if we should let us go. But even then, somehow, it was always us against the problem, not us against each other. We chose each other.

Because you are you, and I am me.
We know we’re not perfect. We know we have our flaws, our moods, our stubbornness, and our crazy little ways.

But inside our little world, somehow, we fit.

Maybe we aren't the perfect couple.
But we are perfectly ours.

And if life gave me the chance to go back and choose again—with everything I know now, every fight, every tear, every laugh, every beautiful memory…

I would still choose you.

Over and over.
Every single time. ❤️
